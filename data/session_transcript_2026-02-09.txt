3. ARC-EASY (MEDIUM complexity)
-------------------------------
Task: Elementary science questions
Config: config/qwen3-0.6B-arc-easy.toml

Results (Step 0 - no ablation):
  - CoT-OFF: 46%
  - CoT-ON:  47%
  - Delta:   +1%
  - Winner:  CoT-ON

Results (Step 1 - 10 tokens ablated):
  - CoT-OFF: 46%
  - CoT-ON:  0%
  - Delta:   -46%

Key Findings:
  - First task where thinking provides benefit
  - BUT: Ablating just 10 tokens dropped CoT-ON from 47% to 0%
  - Those tokens are ESSENTIAL for reasoning
  - Same tokens that hurt on low-complexity tasks ENABLE reasoning here

Critical 10 tokens for ARC-Easy reasoning:
  1. "Okay" - start deliberation
  2. newline
  3. "So" - logical connector
  4. "I" - self-reference
  5. "think" - metacognition
  6. "But" - considering alternatives
  7. "?" - questioning
  8. "First" - planning
  9. "maybe" - uncertainty
  10. "might" - possibility

================================================================================
KEY INSIGHTS - FULL PICTURE EMERGING
================================================================================

1. COMPLEXITY DETERMINES REASONING VALUE (CONFIRMED)
   - LOW complexity: Thinking hurts dramatically (-31% to -51%)
   - MEDIUM complexity: Thinking helps (+1% to +15%)
   - HIGH complexity: Both struggle, thinking slightly hurts (-6%)
   - Pattern FULLY matches Apple ML "Illusion of Thinking" findings

2. SAME TOKENS, OPPOSITE EFFECTS
   - Metacognitive tokens ("Okay", "think", "maybe", "But")
   - HURT on simple tasks (induce unnecessary hesitation)
   - HELP on medium tasks (enable deliberation)
   - OVERWHELM on complex tasks (can't reason through anyway)
   - Ablating them on ARC-Easy: 47% → 0%

3. REASONING IS EXTREMELY FRAGILE (CONFIRMED x2)
   - ARC-Easy: 10 tokens ablated → 47% drops to 0%
   - OpenBookQA: 10 tokens ablated → 45% drops to 0%
   - Pattern is CONSISTENT across MEDIUM complexity tasks
   - Reasoning benefit is ENTIRELY dependent on ~10 critical tokens
   - Suggests reasoning isn't deeply integrated but token-dependent

4. TASK TYPE MATTERS MORE THAN DIFFICULTY
   - Word tasks (unscrambling, hyperbaton) = pattern matching
   - Science QA (ARC, OpenBookQA) = requires connecting facts
   - Logical deduction = requires multi-step inference
   - Pattern matching doesn't benefit from deliberation
   - Multi-step inference exceeds model capacity

5. SWEET SPOT FOR REASONING
   - OpenBookQA shows +15% - largest benefit observed
   - Tasks requiring 1-2 inferential steps benefit most
   - Tasks requiring 0 steps (intuitive) or 3+ steps (complex) don't benefit
