💡 Interpretation

Why Did CoT Hurt on This Run?

Several possible explanations:

  • Random variation: With only 100 examples and 50% baseline, a 2% difference could be statistical noise
  • Task difficulty: This particular subset may favor direct answers
  • Model variance: Same model/seed but different sampling outcomes

The Metacognitive Token Pattern

Despite the performance difference, the distinctive tokens remain consistent:

  • CoT-ON uses deliberation markers ("Okay", "think", "maybe")
  • CoT-OFF provides direct answers without meta-commentary
  • The SAME tokens that hurt here would help on harder reasoning tasks
Core Insight: The metacognitive tokens are task-dependent. They can help OR hurt depending on whether the task benefits from deliberation (medium complexity) or is better solved intuitively (low complexity).
