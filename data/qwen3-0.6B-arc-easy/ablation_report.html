💡 Interpretation

Why Did CoT Hurt on ARC-Easy?

  • Task below CoT threshold: Elementary science questions may not require multi-step deliberation — direct recall is faster and more accurate
  • Statistical noise: −2% on n=100 is within noise (95% CI ≈ ±10%)
  • Overthinking penalty: CoT may introduce errors by second-guessing correct intuitions on low-complexity questions

What the Rising Tokens Tell Us

  • The metacognitive vocabulary forms a dense, tightly-scored cluster — ranks 0–29 span only 0.005 score units
  • Ablating top-10 would likely surface let, Wait, Hmm at step 1, then remember, probably at step 2
  • The reasoning style appears distributed across synonymous tokens, not bottlenecked — complete ablation may require 5–10+ steps
  • On a task where CoT helps (OpenBookQA), these rising tokens are the compensation candidates to track with the RisingTokens viewer
