The Critical 10 Tokens

Based on earlier findings, these metacognitive tokens are ESSENTIAL for reasoning:

  1. "Okay" - start deliberation
  2. newline - structure thinking
  3. "So" - logical connector
  4. "I" - self-reference
  5. "think" - metacognition
  6. "But" - considering alternatives
  7. "?" - questioning
  8. "First" - planning
  9. "maybe" - uncertainty
  10. "might" - possibility
Paradox: The SAME tokens that hurt on low-complexity tasks (causing overthinking) ENABLE reasoning on medium-complexity tasks. This explains why CoT is task-dependent.

🎯 Research Implications

Validates Apple ML "Illusion of Thinking" Hypothesis

Our findings support the research showing that reasoning benefits are:

  • Task-dependent: Helps on medium complexity, hurts on low/high
  • Fragile: Removing ~10 tokens causes complete collapse
  • Token-dependent: Not deeply integrated reasoning but surface-level patterns

Pattern Across Complexity Levels

Complexity Tasks CoT Effect Explanation
LOW Word Unscrambling, Hyperbaton -31% to -51% Overthinking destroys intuition
MEDIUM ARC-Easy, OpenBookQA +1% to +15% Deliberation helps connect facts
HIGH Logical Deduction -6% Both approaches overwhelmed
Sweet Spot: Tasks requiring 1-2 inferential steps benefit most from CoT. Tasks requiring 0 steps (intuitive) or 3+ steps (complex) don't benefit.
