Research Hypothesis

Based on Apple ML Research "The Illusion of Thinking":

  • Low complexity: Direct answers outperform reasoning
  • Medium complexity: Reasoning provides advantage
  • High complexity: Both approaches struggle

Detailed Results

1. Word Unscrambling (LOW complexity) - COMPLETE

Pattern matching task: identify unscrambled word from options.

  • CoT-OFF: 66% - Direct, confident answers
  • CoT-ON: 35% - Overthinking, hesitation, incomplete reasoning
  • Key insight: 3,626 hesitation markers in CoT-ON vs 5 in CoT-OFF

2. Hyperbaton (LOW complexity) - COMPLETE

Word order task: identify sentence with correct adjective ordering.

  • CoT-OFF: 55% - Direct grammatical intuition
  • CoT-ON: 4% - Catastrophic failure, thinking destroys performance
  • Key insight: -51% delta is the largest observed so far
  • This task tests linguistic intuition, not reasoning

3. ARC-Easy (MEDIUM complexity) - COMPLETE

Elementary science questions requiring basic reasoning.

  • CoT-OFF: 46% - Direct factual recall
  • CoT-ON: 47% - Thinking provides marginal benefit (+1%)
  • Critical finding: Ablating just 10 tokens dropped CoT-ON from 47% to 0%
  • Those 10 tokens were essential for ALL reasoning benefit
  • This suggests reasoning is fragile - a few key tokens enable it entirely

4. OpenBookQA (MEDIUM complexity) - COMPLETE

Science questions requiring external knowledge and reasoning.

  • CoT-OFF: 30% - Direct factual recall often insufficient
  • CoT-ON: 45% - Thinking provides +15% benefit (largest observed!)
  • Key insight: Tasks requiring 1-2 inferential steps benefit most from reasoning
  • This is the "sweet spot" for chain-of-thought

⚠️ Ablation Result: After removing just 10 tokens, CoT-ON dropped from 45% to 0%! Same pattern as ARC-Easy - reasoning benefit is entirely dependent on a small set of critical tokens.

5. Logical Deduction (HIGH complexity) - COMPLETE

Multi-step logical ordering of objects based on constraints.

  • CoT-OFF: 18% - Near random (expected for 5-choice)
  • CoT-ON: 12% - Thinking actually hurts (-6%)
  • Key insight: Task exceeds model's reasoning capacity
  • Deliberation cannot overcome fundamental capability limits
  • Matches Apple ML finding: complex tasks overwhelm reasoning

Conclusions

The Reasoning Sweet Spot:

Chain-of-thought reasoning is not universally beneficial. It helps most on medium complexity tasks that require 1-2 inferential steps but don't overwhelm the model's capacity. Simple pattern-matching tasks are harmed by overthinking, while highly complex tasks exceed what deliberation can solve.


Reasoning is Extremely Fragile:

On both MEDIUM complexity tasks (ARC-Easy and OpenBookQA), ablating just 10 tokens caused performance to collapse from ~45% to 0%. This suggests that reasoning benefit is entirely dependent on a tiny set of metacognitive tokens - it's not deeply integrated into model behavior.


Practical Implications:

  • Disable thinking for intuitive/pattern tasks (word games, grammar)
  • Enable thinking for science QA, factual reasoning
  • Don't rely on thinking for multi-step logical deduction
  • Reasoning can be completely disabled by masking ~10 key tokens
